# Wallet Manager — Role v0.1

## Purpose

Help people safely receive, send, and understand digital value.

Reduce mistakes.  
Build confidence.  
Support movement inside OpenSourceCity.

---

## What This Role Does

A Wallet Manager helps someone:

1. Receive value  
2. Send value  
3. Check and confirm transactions  

This role does NOT take control of anyone’s wallet.

---

## Core Rule (Non-Negotiable)

> Never ask for or store private keys, seed phrases, or passwords.

If someone shares them:
→ tell them to secure their wallet immediately

---

## The 3 Core Actions

### 1. Receiving

Guide the user to:
- Find their wallet address  
- Share it safely  
- Confirm receipt  

---

### 2. Sending

Guide the user to:
- Enter the correct address  
- Double-check it  
- Send a small test amount first  

---

### 3. Confirming

Teach them to:
- View the transaction  
- Understand success/failure  
- Use a block explorer if needed  

---

## Script (Use or Adapt)

> “I’ll guide you, but you stay in control.  
> We’ll receive, send, and confirm—step by step.”

---

## Tools

- walletmanagers.org (hub)
- howtoshopweb3.com (supporting guide)
- Block explorers (as needed)

---

## Safety Practices

- Always suggest test transactions first  
- Never rush the process  
- Encourage users to write down and secure their wallet info offline  
- Remind users: control = responsibility  

---

## Time Target

10–20 minutes per session.

---

## Compensation Path

Early:
- Recognition  
- Random Rain  

Mid:
- Tips or small service payments  

Later:
- Trusted Wallet Manager role  
- Paid assistance  

---

## Day 1 Task

Help ONE person:
- Receive a small amount  
- Send a small amount  
- Confirm the transaction  

Ensure they understand what happened.

---

## Record Format (simple)

- Date  
- Person helped (initials ok)  
- Action completed  
- Any issues resolved  

---

## What This Role Is NOT

- Not a bank  
- Not a custodian  
- Not a controller of funds  

---

## Closing Thought

You are not moving money.

You are helping someone:
> understand how to move value on their own
