# Content Builder — Role v0.1

## Purpose

Create simple, useful, and repeatable content that helps people understand and participate in OpenSourceCity.

Content Builders turn ideas into:
- Guides
- Stories
- Images
- Records
- Instructions

---

## What This Role Does

A Content Builder helps:

1. Explain the system  
2. Teach new users  
3. Record ideas and progress  
4. Translate complex ideas into simple language  

This role feeds the entire city.

---

## Main Areas of Content

### 1. Education
Examples:
- Wallet setup
- How to receive value
- Web3 basics

---

### 2. Storytelling
Examples:
- OpenSourceCity storybook pages
- Builder journeys
- “Why this matters” content

---

### 3. Documentation
Examples:
- GitHub records
- IPFS logs
- City updates
- Role descriptions

---

### 4. Visual Content
Examples:
- Maps
- Diagrams
- Trigger images
- City layouts

---

## Core Principle

> “If people cannot understand it, they cannot build it.”

Keep things:
- Simple
- Clear
- Human

---

## Tools

- OpenSourceCity.art
- 1stdomainpublishingco.crypto
- GitHub
- IPFS
- Images
- Simple diagrams

---

## Recommended Style

Explain things as if:
- Someone is intelligent
- But seeing this world for the first time

Avoid:
- Unnecessary jargon
- Complexity for appearance
- Talking over people

---

## Suggested Workflow

1. Observe an idea
2. Simplify the idea
3. Record the idea
4. Publish the idea
5. Allow others to build from it

---

## Compensation Path

Early:
- Attribution
- Proof of contribution
- Public recognition

Mid:
- Paid guides
- Content requests
- Sponsored educational material

Later:
- Publishing role
- Revenue share
- Educational leadership

---

## Day 1 Task

Create ONE useful thing:

Examples:
- A simple guide
- A story page
- A map
- A visual explanation
- A glossary entry

Publish it publicly.

---

## Record Format (simple)

- Date
- Content created
- Purpose
- Link or CID if available

---

## Rule

> “Perfect content that never ships teaches no one.”

Publish simple. Improve later.

---

## Closing Thought

You are not creating content for attention alone.

You are helping:
> ideas move from one mind into many
