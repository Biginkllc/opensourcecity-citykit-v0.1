# Onboarding Guide — Role v0.1

## Purpose

Help a new person enter OpenSourceCity in under 15 minutes.

Remove confusion.  
Reduce fear.  
Get them to their first successful action.

---

## What This Role Does

An Onboarding Guide helps someone:

1. Set up a wallet  
2. Understand basic terms  
3. Complete their first simple action  

That’s it.

No overload. No complexity.

---

## The 3-Step Method (Always Use This)

### Step 1 — Wallet
Help them install or access a wallet.

Say:
> “This is your key. You control it.”

---

### Step 2 — Funding
Explain how value gets into the wallet.

Keep it simple:
- Send from another wallet  
- Use an exchange  
- Receive a small test amount  

---

### Step 3 — First Action
Have them do ONE action:

- Receive something  
- Send something  
- View a transaction  

Success = confidence.

---

## Script (Use or Adapt)

> “I’ll walk you through this step-by-step.  
> We’ll do three things: set up your wallet, get value into it, and make your first move.  
> After that, you’re in.”
