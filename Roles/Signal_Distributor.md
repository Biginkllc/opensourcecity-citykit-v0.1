# Signal Distributor — Role v0.1

## Purpose

Help people discover OpenSourceCity, understand its purpose, and find their place inside it.

A Signal Distributor spreads:
- awareness
- entry points
- opportunities
- useful ideas

This role helps the city grow.

---

## What This Role Does

A Signal Distributor helps:

1. Bring attention to the system
2. Share useful entry points
3. Direct people toward learning and participation
4. Reduce fear and confusion through simple communication

This role acts as:
> the voice, signs, and pathways of the city

---

## Main Responsibilities

### 1. Share Entry Points

Examples:
- howtoshopweb3.com
- opensourcecity.art
- paxgdrops.com

---

### 2. Share Visual Signals

Examples:
- maps
- story images
- diagrams
- concept visuals
- educational graphics

---

### 3. Share Real Examples

Examples:
- successful onboarding
- shop setups
- Random Rain demonstrations
- builder stories

---

### 4. Invite Participation

Encourage people to:
- learn
- contribute
- build
- help others

---

## Core Principle

> “Confused people do not move.”

Keep communication:
- simple
- visual
- direct
- human

---

## Recommended Style

Focus on:
- clarity
- usefulness
- possibility

Avoid:
- unnecessary hype
- fear-based messaging
- overwhelming technical language

---

## Tools

- Social platforms
- Domains
- Images
- Maps
- GitHub links
- Story content
- Simple explanations

---

## Suggested Workflow

1. Observe a useful idea or action
2. Simplify it
3. Share it publicly
4. Direct people toward the correct entry point
5. Repeat consistently

---

## Day 1 Task

Create ONE signal.

Examples:
- A post
- A diagram
- A short explanation
- A visual
- A link pathway

The signal should answer:
> “Why should someone care?”

---

## Record Format (simple)

- Date
- Signal shared
- Purpose
- Link or platform used

---

## Rule

> “A small clear signal is stronger than a large confusing one.”
